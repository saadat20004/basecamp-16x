The trailer

Built for dirt roads, comfy enough for long weekends.

This is a beautifully kept Airstream Basecamp 16X. If you haven't met one before, the X is the overland, off road edition of the popular Basecamp line: higher clearance, tougher build, same clever layout.

Everything you need for a campground stay or a backroad adventure comes along for the ride. Propane for the stove, water heater and furnace is on the house, so you never pay for refills.
